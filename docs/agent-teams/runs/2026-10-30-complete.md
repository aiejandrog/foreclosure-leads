# 2026-10-30 complete (callmode-dev): "Complete" step before the lead advances

## OUTPUT-TEMPLATE
**What changed (plain English).** Pressing Next on a lead card (`#skip`) or Next lead on the post-outcome screen (`#nx`) now opens a sheet, "What did you do on this lead?", before the lead advances. Chips (tick several): Called, Texted, Emailed, Left a voicemail, Mailed a letter, Knocked on the door, Spoke to someone else (son / daughter / spouse / relative / neighbour / other, plus a note), Other (note). Buttons: "Log N and next lead" (reads "Next lead" when nothing new is ticked, so one tap advances) and "Skip, log nothing". Anything already logged today on that case (a call touch or a tapped dial outcome, a text touch, an email touch, a voicemail outcome, a letter, a door) is shown ticked and locked, so nothing is logged twice. A tapped-but-never-tagged (`pending`) dial does not lock Called. A do-not-contact / opted-out / dead lead gets no sheet and nothing is logged on it. Not wired (nothing to log): the teammate-takeover `#rcgo` and the blocked-lead `#skip`. The open-deal "set the next step date" guard on `#nx` still answers first.

**What each chip writes.** One `n.touches.push({d, ts, tsu, ch, out, src:'manual', mk, by})`, then ONE `saveNotes()` for the whole Log tap (plus the same `queueSync()` the pasted-email and text code call).
| chip | ch | out |
|---|---|---|
| Called | call | Called (logged by hand) |
| Texted | text | Texted (logged by hand) |
| Emailed | email | Emailed (logged by hand) |
| Left a voicemail | call | Left a voicemail (logged by hand) |
| Mailed a letter | letter | Mailed a letter (logged by hand) |
| Knocked on the door | door | Knocked on the door (logged by hand) |
| Spoke to someone else | other | Spoke to <who> (logged by hand) - note; also `who`, `note` |
| Other | other | Other (logged by hand) - note; also `note` |

`letter` and `door` were already in `OUTREACH_CH`, so the page reads them safely. `other` is new and deliberately NOT in `OUTREACH_CH`: it appears in the call log and `contactHistory` but does not count as outreach, does not move a lead out of Untouched, does not trigger the soft cooldown, and does not count toward FTSA. Reason: speaking to a son is not contact with the owner, and treating it as outreach to the owner would hide the owner from the queue.

**Where it shows.** Call log (`screenRegistry`): extended to list any lead with a `src:'manual'` touch of any channel (title now "people logged"). "Reached out": a manual call / text / email / letter / door is read by the existing `lastOutreach` / `_contactTier` / `_viewOf`, so the lead leaves Untouched (proved in the test). Side effects that follow from the existing rules, not new code: a manual call touch starts the normal call cooldown (`suppressed`, via `lastCall`); a manual text/email/letter/door starts the soft "reached another way" hold (`supReason`, COOL_DEFAULT_H). Both are the safe direction.

**Hard limits kept.** No dial, conversation, appointment, stage or `status`; no change to no / dnc / notint, badph / dntph, optout, the DNC ledger or send_server.py. "Spoke to someone else" is only a note: no phone number is added or made callable, nothing goes in `wpRelatives` (a number typed into the free-text note is stored as text only, never parsed). A manual Texted does NOT post `/text` and does NOT write text_sent.json. A manual Emailed does NOT write mail_sent.json, so the nightly cadence cannot see it: **cadence may still email a lead the operator already emailed by hand; the touch is only visible in Call Mode and the board.** FTSA: `_tele24n` counts call and text touches, so a manual Called / Texted / voicemail counts toward the 3-in-24h cap; that is the safe direction (it can only block more).
