3. No retainer — $0 due at signing

Client pays nothing upfront. No retainer, consultation fee, filing fee advance, or deposit is due upon signing this Agreement. Client’s only payment obligation under this Agreement is the Contingent Fee described in Section 5, and only if Success (or Partial Success) occurs.

4. Definitions of Success and Recovered Amount

  1. “Recovered Amount” means money that is actually received by Client (or by a jointly controlled escrow / court clerk disbursement clearly for Client’s benefit) as a direct result of recovering all or part of the Target Amount that would otherwise have been lost, taken, withheld, or paid to the opposing party identified above.
  2. “Success” means Recovered Amount of at least $19,000.00 attributable to the Target Amount.
  3. “Partial Success” means Recovered Amount greater than $0.00 but less than $19,000.00 attributable to the Target Amount.
  4. Money Client was already entitled to receive independently of recovering the Target Amount (for example, a separate surplus balance already awarded to Client or Client’s assignee that is unrelated to clawing back the Target Amount) is not Recovered Amount under this Agreement and does not trigger the Contingent Fee.

5. Contingent fee (recovery-then-pay)

  1. Full Success: If Recovered Amount equals $19,000.00 (or more solely as to the Target Amount), Client keeps $10,000.00 of that Recovered Amount and pays Company a Contingent Fee of $9,000.00 (the “Fee Cap”).
  2. Partial Success (pro-rata — mandatory fairness rule): Contingent Fee = Recovered Amount × (9 ÷ 19). Client keeps Recovered Amount × (10 ÷ 19). Example: if $9,500 is recovered, Client keeps $5,000.00 and Company fee is $4,500.00.
  3. Fee Cap: Company’s Contingent Fee shall never exceed $9,000.00, even if Recovered Amount exceeds $19,000.00.
  4. Zero recovery: If Recovered Amount is $0.00, Client owes $0.00. Both sides absorb their own time and opportunity cost. No fee, no penalty, no “work performed” invoice.
  5. When fee is due: Contingent Fee is due only after the Recovered Amount is actually received by Client or jointly controlled escrow, and only from that Recovered Amount (or an equivalent immediate remittance). Client never owes an out-of-pocket fee if nothing is recovered.
  6. Payment method: Prefer simultaneous split at disbursement / escrow. If paid to Client first, Client shall remit Company’s Contingent Fee within five (5) business days of Client’s receipt of the Recovered Amount.

6. Company costs

Unless the parties agree otherwise in a separate signed writing, Company pays its own ordinary costs of the Recovery Effort (travel, copies, skip-trace tools, administrative time, etc.). Company will not charge Client cost reimbursements contingent on failure. Extraordinary third-party costs (for example, licensed attorney retainers, filing fees paid to a court, or expert fees) will be incurred only if Client authorizes them in writing; those authorized third-party costs are Client’s responsibility to the vendor and are separate from the Contingent Fee.

7. What Company will do — and what Company does not promise

Company will use commercially reasonable efforts, which may include (as applicable and lawful):

  • organizing facts, documents, timelines, and money math for Client;
  • communicating with non-attorney third parties, title / escrow contacts, or opposing parties’ representatives where permitted;
  • helping Client update mailing / disbursement information with the clerk or disbursing office;
  • referring Client to independent licensed Florida counsel if legal advocacy, court filings, or negotiation of stipulated court orders is required;
  • coordinating logistics around any recovery or escrow split once funds move.
Not a law firm / no guarantee. Miami Solutions Group is not a law firm and is not providing legal services, legal advice, court representation, or attorney-client privilege. Company does not guarantee any recovery, any court outcome, or any timeline. If a court filing, motion practice, stipulation challenge, or other legal advocacy is required, that work must be performed by a lawyer licensed in Florida whom Client may hire separately. Company’s fee under this Agreement is a consulting / recovery-assistance fee on funds actually recovered for Client — not a lawyer contingent fee.

8. Client responsibilities

  • Provide truthful information and available documents promptly;
  • Maintain a working mailing address and phone number for court / clerk notices;
  • Execute reasonable authorizations needed for information access or escrow instructions;
  • Not settle, assign, or redirect the Target Amount in a way that defeats Company’s earned Contingent Fee after Success/Partial Success without first satisfying Section 5;
  • Decide whether to hire independent counsel; Company will not interfere with that choice.

9. Termination

  1. Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice (including email or signed letter).
  2. If terminated before any Recovered Amount is received, Client owes $0.00.
  3. If Success or Partial Success occurs after termination but is substantially the result of Company’s Recovery Effort begun before termination, the Contingent Fee in Section 5 still applies to that Recovered Amount.
  4. Sections 5, 10, 11, and 12 survive termination as needed to enforce earned fees and the parties’ acknowledgments.
