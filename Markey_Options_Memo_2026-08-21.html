1. The emergency, and the free tool nobody has used

Case COWE-26-055152, Phoenician Cove HOA, LP recorded 7/23/2026, not yet served. Claim of lien $3,207.00 (instr 120889339).

⚠ Homestead does NOT protect them. Art. X §4(a) Fla. Const. exempts homestead except for “taxes and assessments thereon,” and Florida treats a declaration-based assessment lien as consensual, relating back to the declaration (Bessemer v. Gersten). An $839,540 homestead can be sold over $3,207. And if it sells, the first mortgage SURVIVES — the buyer takes subject to it, bids only the equity spread, and the Markeys would likely be handed a large surplus check and evicted from the house Kevin says he will not leave. That is the actual nightmare scenario in this file.
⚑ THE MOVE: Fla. Stat. § 720.3085(6) — “QUALIFYING OFFER.” A written offer to pay all amounts secured by the lien, filed with the court, is a self-executing automatic stay of up to 60 days — and it freezes the association’s attorney’s fees while it runs. Caption it “QUALIFYING OFFER — AUTOMATIC STAY INVOKED PURSUANT TO F.S. 720.3085.” Must be signed by all owners AND the homestead spouse — so Kevin and Donna both sign.

It is available today only because nothing else is pending. It is unavailable if the parcel is the subject of a mortgage foreclosure or tax certificate sale, or if they file bankruptcy — and it becomes voidable if any of those start while it is pending. Use it before anything else attaches.
⚠ DO NOT MAIL THEM $3,207. Under § 720.3085(3)(b) payments apply first to interest, then late fees, then costs and attorney’s fees — and to the delinquent assessment LAST. Send the face amount and the assessments are still unpaid, the lien is still alive, and the case is still running. Cure only to a written, dated payoff figure.

Get it via an estoppel certificate, § 720.30851 — due in 10 business days, fees capped ($299 base, +$179 delinquent, +$119 to expedite to 3 days), and binding for 30 days. If they miss the 10-day deadline, no fee may be charged. That converts “$3,207 plus unknown fees” into a hard number you can wire against.

Realistic all-in cure: $5,000–$8,500. $3,207 + 18%/yr simple (~$577/yr; HB 1203 banned compounding after 7/1/2024 — audit the ledger) + association counsel fees on a filed-but-unserved county case (typically $1,500–$3,000) + costs. Fees are at their lowest point they will ever be right now. The urgency is fee-driven, not sale-driven.

Three fee defenses if they cut corners: §720.3085(3) requires a written Notice of Late Assessment (30 days) before any fees are collectible; §(4) a 45-day Notice of Intent to Record a Claim of Lien; §(5) a separate 45-day Notice of Intent to Foreclose. Skip one and the fees built on it are not collectible. Also §720.305(2): fines under $1,000 cannot be in the lien — audit the $3,207 line by line. Owner-side fee authority: Ocean Two Condo Ass’n v. Kliger, 3d DCA 2008 (duty to mitigate; foreclosure over $25 dismissed as premature). Timeline: plaintiff has 120 days from 7/23/2026 to serve — about 11/20/2026.
