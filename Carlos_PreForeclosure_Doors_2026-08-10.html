Carlos — PRE-FORECLOSURE doors

Built 2026-08-10 · these are JUST-FILED foreclosures. No auction date yet. This is the earliest and best moment to knock — the owner still has every option open.

💬 THE SCRIPT — say it like this

KNOCK: "Good morning, are you [Owner name]? My name is Carlos, I'm with Miami Solutions Group. I'm not here to sell you anything and I'm not from the bank."
WHY YOU ARE THERE: "The bank filed a foreclosure case on this house in the county records. There's no auction date yet, which is actually good news, that's why I came now instead of later."
THE ONE QUESTION: "What happened with the house?" — then STOP TALKING. Let them tell you. That answer is the whole visit.
WHAT WE DO: "There are five ways out of this: catch up what's behind, a loan modification, a short sale, sell it privately before it ever goes to auction, or bankruptcy. Which one fits depends on your numbers. Jose on our team does this every day and it costs you nothing to talk."
CLOSE: "What's the best number for Jose to call you? He'll walk you through what still works." — get the number, that's the win. You are NOT closing a deal at the door.

⚠ NEVER SAY

  • Never "I can stop the foreclosure" or "we can save your house" — that is the one promise Florida law punishes (FS 501.1377 / MARS Rule).
  • Never ask for money. Not now, not ever at the door. We charge nothing up front, period.
  • Never say we are the bank, a lawyer, a government agency, or a "rescue" company.
  • Never quote a dollar amount you did not read off the card. If they ask what they owe: "I don't want to guess, Jose will pull the exact figures."
  • If they say stop / not interested / don't come back — you leave, and text Alex so it's recorded. That address never gets contacted again.

✅ WHAT TO BRING BACK

  • Best phone number + best time to call
  • Their answer to "what happened"
  • Are they still living there? Anyone else on the deed? Is it listed with a realtor?
  • Nobody home → leave the door hanger / card, mark it, move on. Do not talk to neighbors about their foreclosure.
