bsg-logo.svg is the OFFICIAL BSG mark, chosen by Alejandro 2026-08-28.

It is the vector in bsg_mark.py: skyline, arc, BSG monogram, and the BISCAYNE / SOLUTIONS GROUP
wordmark as real type. Everything taking --logo picks it up from here automatically.

Why vector and not the old PNG: the chrome "BSG FLORIDA" badge printed "FLORIDA" at a 1.5-2.5pt
cap height (6pt is the floor), had no working single-colour version -- inverting it produced an
unreadable white blob -- and its alpha edges were matted toward black, so it haloed on white
stock. This one reverses cleanly, stays legible at 0.3in, and has no resolution to run out of.

To replace it later, drop a new file here with the same name. Any of .svg .png .jpg works.
