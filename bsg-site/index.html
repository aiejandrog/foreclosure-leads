How we can help

Four paths, depending on what the records say

Which one fits depends on numbers most homeowners have never been shown — what is actually owed, who actually holds the note, and how much time the court file really gives you.

Reinstatement

Find the real number to bring the loan or HOA current — including fees most payoff letters bury — and the deadline it has to be paid by.

Sell with time to spare

If keeping it doesn't pencil out, selling before a judgment protects your equity. We'll make a cash offer or refer you to an agent — whichever nets you more.

Short sale support

When the debt is larger than the value, we assemble the package the servicer asks for and keep the file moving.

Attorney referral

Bankruptcy and litigation are legal work and we don't do legal work. When that's the right answer, we say so and connect you with a Florida attorney.

What actually makes us different

We read the file before we say a word

Most companies that mail you got your address off a list and know nothing else about your situation. Before we ever give you an opinion, we pull the recorded instruments from the county and read them. It costs you nothing and it is the whole difference.

  • The lien itself — not the index summary. County indexes routinely mislabel what a document does; the paper says something different more often than you'd think.
  • Who really holds the note today — loans get sold and reassigned, and the company mailing you is often not the one you'd have to deal with.
  • The real clock — whether you've been served, how long the plaintiff has, and what deadline actually applies to you.
  • What it would truly cost to fix — the payoff figure with fees and interest, not the number printed on the first notice.
