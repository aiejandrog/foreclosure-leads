NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein, the parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. TERM AND POSSESSION

1.1 **Term.** Occupant shall have the right to occupy the Property for a period of **___ days** commencing on the date of closing (the "Occupancy Period"), not to exceed **90 days** without written attorney review.

1.2 **Surrender.** Occupant shall surrender possession of the Property, vacant and broom-clean, no later than 11:59 PM on the last day of the Occupancy Period.

1.3 **Holdover.** If Occupant fails to surrender possession by the expiration of the Occupancy Period, Occupant shall be a **tenant at sufferance** (not a statutory tenant) and shall pay holdover damages of **$___ per day** (not less than 150% of the daily Occupancy Fee defined in §2). Purchaser may pursue eviction under **FS 66.021** (unlawful detainer) or **FS 83.20** (if a tenancy is deemed created by a court), without further notice.

---

## 2. OCCUPANCY FEE (NOT RENT)

2.1 **Fee.** In exchange for the right to occupy the Property during the Occupancy Period, Occupant shall pay to Purchaser an **Occupancy Fee** of **$___ per month**, due in advance on the first day of each month. The first payment is due at closing.

2.2 **Not Rent.** The parties expressly acknowledge and agree that:
   - (a) This agreement is **not a lease** under **FS 83** or any other statute;
   - (b) The Occupancy Fee is **not rent** and does not create a landlord-tenant relationship;
   - (c) Occupant is a **former owner in holdover possession**, not a tenant;
   - (d) No security deposit is collected and no tenant protections apply.

2.3 **Default.** Failure to pay the Occupancy Fee within **5 days** of its due date constitutes a material breach. Purchaser may terminate this agreement immediately and pursue eviction.

---

## 3. UTILITIES AND MAINTENANCE

3.1 **Utilities.** Occupant shall maintain all utility services (electric, water, gas, sewer, trash) in Occupant's name and shall not allow any service to be disconnected.

3.2 **Maintenance.** Occupant shall maintain the Property in substantially the same condition as existed at closing, reasonable wear and tear excepted. Occupant shall not make alterations, additions, or improvements without Purchaser's prior written consent.

3.3 **Insurance.** Purchaser shall maintain property insurance. Occupant is encouraged to maintain renter's insurance but is not required to do so.

---

## 4. USE RESTRICTIONS

4.1 **Residential Only.** The Property shall be used solely as Occupant's personal residence. No subletting, Airbnb, commercial use, or assignment.

4.2 **No New Liens.** Occupant shall not encumber the Property with any lien, mortgage, or mechanic's lien. Any lien filed by Occupant or Occupant's contractors shall be void and Occupant shall indemnify Purchaser.

4.3 **No Further Claims.** Occupant waives any claim of homestead exemption, tenant rights, or equitable relief that would delay Purchaser's recovery of possession. This waiver is knowing and voluntary, supported by separate consideration (the relocation assistance and transition time).

---

## 5. RELOCATION ASSISTANCE

5.1 **Assistance.** Purchaser shall provide Occupant with **relocation assistance** of **$___** at closing, to be used for moving expenses, deposits, and transition costs. This is a **one-time payment**, not a recurring obligation.

5.2 **Movers.** Purchaser may, at its option, arrange for professional movers at Purchaser's expense. If so, Occupant shall cooperate with scheduling and packing.

---

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

6.1 **No Duress.** Occupant represents that they have entered this agreement freely and voluntarily, without coercion, undue influence, or fraud. Occupant has had the opportunity to consult with independent legal counsel.

6.2 **Capacity.** Occupant represents that they are **not under a guardianship**, **not subject to a power of attorney** held by Purchaser or its affiliates, and have full legal capacity to contract.

6.3 **Age Acknowledgment.** [IF OCCUPANT IS 65+: ] Occupant acknowledges that they are age ___ and that Purchaser has advised them of their right to seek independent legal counsel before signing this agreement. Purchaser has provided a **3-business-day rescission period** per **FS 501.1377(3)(c)**.

---

## 7. COMPLIANCE WITH FS 501.1377

7.1 **Disclosures.** If the underlying transaction was an **equity purchase** from a homestead owner in foreclosure, Purchaser has provided Occupant with all disclosures required by **FS 501.1377(3)**, including:
   - (a) The 3-business-day right of rescission;
   - (b) The notice of cancellation rights;
   - (c) The written contract with all mandatory terms;
   - (d) The 82% equity-purchase price floor (if applicable).

7.2 **Verification.** A copy of the FS 501.1377 disclosure package is attached as **Exhibit A**.

8.2 **Attorney's Fees.** The prevailing party in any litigation shall be entitled to reasonable attorney's fees and costs.
