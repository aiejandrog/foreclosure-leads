"Quick thing before we start — I record my calls so I keep your file straight. That okay with you?"

Read that out loud first. A no means stop recording, not hang up — press the second button and the coach runs with no mic and nothing written to disk.

Audio goes to call_sessions/<stamp>_<case>/audio.webm on this laptop, in five-second chunks, so a crash costs you five seconds. Nothing leaves this machine.

Transcript is Chrome's recognizer — that audio is processed by Google's speech service. The text lands in transcript.txt when you stop.

Speakerphone only. Neither phone OS hands call audio to an app. Phone on speaker, laptop mic listening. It hears both sides and cannot tell your voice from theirs — the red bar means "heard on the line," not "you said."
