BSG Call Coach

Recorder · instant transcript · NEPQ stage coach · objection auto-fire

"Quick thing before we start — I record my calls so I keep your file straight. That okay with you?"

Read that out loud first. A no means stop recording, not hang up — press the second button and the coach runs with no mic and nothing written to disk.

Audio goes to call_sessions/<stamp>_<case>/audio.webm on this laptop, in five-second chunks, so a crash costs you five seconds. Nothing leaves this machine.

Transcript is Chrome's recognizer (audio is processed by Google's speech service) — or Deepgram when a key is saved, which adds real speaker labels. The text lands in transcript.txt when you stop.

Speakerphone only. Neither phone OS hands call audio to an app. Phone on speaker, laptop mic listening. Without a Deepgram key, YOU?/THEM? labels are a best guess by content.
